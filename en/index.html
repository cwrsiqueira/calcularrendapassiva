How Much Do I Need to Invest for Passive Income?

The quick formula is monthly income × 12 ÷ annual yield. With the 4% rule, that means 300 times the monthly income you want: $1,000 a month needs about $300,000, and $5,000 a month needs about $1.5 million. The lower the yield, the more you need invested — but savings rates move with interest rates, while stock dividends and the 4% rule are designed to keep up with inflation.

How Long Does It Take to Reach Your Goal?

Time is the variable that matters most. Investing $1,000 a month at a 10% average annual return (the long-term stock market average), you reach $1 million in about 23 years; at 7% a year (after inflation) it takes about 28 years. Best Investments for Passive Income in 2026

The most popular passive income sources are dividend ETFs like SCHD, VYM, and JEPI (current yields 3–7%); REITs with monthly distributions; dividend stocks from established companies; and bonds and Treasury securities for stable fixed income.
