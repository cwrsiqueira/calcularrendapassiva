It compounds your balance month by month using reference rates updated every month.

How much do I need to invest to make $1,000 a month in passive income?

About $300,000 using the 4% rule, about $286,000 in a high-yield savings account at 4.2% APY, or about $324,000 in a dividend ETF yielding 3.7% (October 2026 rates, before taxes).

How much do I need to invest to make $5,000 a month?

About $1.5 million using the 4% rule. At 4.2% in savings or T-bills you would need around $1.43 million, and around $1.62 million in a dividend ETF yielding 3.7%.

How long does it take to build $1 million investing $1,000 a month?

At a 10% average annual return (the long-term S&P 500 average), investing $1,000 a month reaches $1 million in about 23 years. At 7% a year it takes about 28 years.

What is the 4% rule?

The 4% rule says you can withdraw 4% of your portfolio in the first year of retirement and adjust that amount for inflation each year with a low risk of running out of money over 30 years. To find the portfolio you need, multiply your annual spending by 25.

How Much Do I Need to Invest for Passive Income?

The quick formula is monthly income × 12 ÷ annual yield. With the 4% rule, that means 300 times the monthly income you want: $1,000 a month needs about $300,000, and $5,000 a month needs about $1.5 million. The lower the yield, the more you need invested — but savings rates move with interest rates, while stock dividends and the 4% rule are designed to keep up with inflation.
